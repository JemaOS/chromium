5. Specific Commitments: telemetry, local AI and no advertising

Unlike some companies in the sector, JEMA does not integrate any intrusive telemetry by default in its software. Telemetry (i.e., the automatic collection of usage data from your device or software) is disabled by default on JemaOS, Flow, Thot and our other products. Concretely, this means that your usage data is not transmitted to our servers without action or consent on your part. For example, JemaOS does not report usage statistics of your applications, and Thot does not send your browsing history to JEMA. The only outgoing communications from our software are limited to what is necessary for the operation of the service you have requested (e.g., system update check, synchronization you have triggered, connection to your cloud space, etc.), and these exchanges are designed to minimize the data transmitted (only essential technical information, never unnecessary personal content). JEMA favors local processing of user data, particularly for Artificial Intelligence (AI) features integrated into our products. Thanks to the architecture of JemaOS and JEMA devices (equipped notably with AI computing modules – e.g., NPU, Neural Processing Unit), AI models run locally on your device without requiring the transfer of your data to our servers or external cloud services. For example, if JemaOS or Thot includes an intelligent assistant or completion function, the calculations are performed directly on your machine: your files, queries or interactions therefore remain confidential and localized. This approach guarantees maximum confidentiality, as your personal data does not leave your environment for training or executing AI algorithms. In addition, this offers better control of your information (no dependence on an internet connection for these functions, and no third-party access). JEMA is thus committed to a digital sovereignty approach by avoiding sending sensitive data to remote platforms.

JEMA ensures that none of the personal data collected is sold, rented or exploited for advertising purposes. We do not display targeted third-party advertisements in our applications and do not use your data for marketing profiling aimed at behavioral advertising. Similarly, we do not share your information with advertisers or advertising networks without your explicit consent. Our business model is based on our products (device sales, Pro subscriptions, etc.), and not on the monetization of user data. You will therefore not see personalized advertising banners appear as a result of using our services, and you will not receive commercial solicitations from external partners from JEMA without having consented to it. 9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website, web applications and certain online services use cookies and similar technologies (e.g., web beacons, local storage, etc.) to improve your user experience and ensure the proper functioning of services. This section describes JEMA's cookie policy, in accordance with regulatory requirements (GDPR and "ePrivacy" directive transposed into French law).

9.1 What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on the user's terminal (computer, smartphone, etc.) when visiting a website or using an application. Cookies allow the site or application to recognize the terminal during subsequent visits, to record information about browsing (e.g., language preferences, content viewed, session identifier) and to improve the service provided. Other similar technologies may be used for similar purposes, such as Local Storage (browser local storage), invisible pixels or web beacons embedded in a page, etc. In this Policy, we refer to all of these technologies as "cookies" for simplicity.

9.2 Types of cookies used

We use different types of cookies, classified according to their purpose. JEMA favors a reasonable use of trackers: most of our cookies are intended to ensure the technical provision of the service or to remember your choices, and we limit the use of optional cookies. The categories of cookies that may be placed via our services are as follows:

Strictly necessary cookies: these cookies are essential for the correct functioning of our sites or applications. Without them, certain requested features could not be provided. For example, these are session cookies that maintain your connection when you access a secure area (your JEMA account), cookies allowing load balancing between our servers, or cookies retaining your essential preferences (such as the display language) when this is technically necessary. These cookies do not require your prior consent, as they are purely functional and proportionate to the requested service.

Functionality cookies (preferences): these cookies improve the user-friendliness of our services by remembering your non-essential choices and preferences from one session to the next. For example, a cookie may remember certain of your interface settings, the last service viewed, or your choice to disable/enable an optional feature. They can also be used to pre-fill certain form fields to save you from retyping your information each time. The activation of these cookies is intended to save you time and personalize your experience. They may be placed by JEMA or by third-party providers integrated into our services (e.g., a support chat module). These preference cookies are generally subject to consent if their refusal has little impact on the service, and you can disable them at any time (see 9.3).

Analytics and performance cookies: these cookies help us understand how our site or application is used, in order to improve its performance and content. This may include, for example, cookies telling us the most visited pages, the time spent on each, navigation paths, or any error messages. JEMA may use privacy-respecting analytics tools (favoring anonymity or self-hosting, without transfer to unsecured third parties). By default, no analytical cookie is placed without your consent. If you accept them, these cookies will collect aggregated information that is not intended to personally identify you but to identify overall trends. They allow us, for example, to detect that a new feature is little used (which could mean that it is not very visible or not intuitive enough). We use this feedback for continuous improvement purposes. You are free to refuse these cookies without major impact on the service.

Advertising and social network cookies: To date, JEMA does not integrate third-party advertising cookies on its own sites and services (see Section 5.3 on the absence of advertising exploitation). We do not display sponsored content from external ad networks, and therefore do not use advertising targeting cookies. Similarly, we do not use social network plugins that would place their own cookies (such as Facebook's "Like" button or LinkedIn tracking) without action on your part. If one day we were to conduct advertising campaigns or integrate such content, this would only be done with your consent and in compliance with regulations (e.g., via a specific cookie banner). Outside of our own site, if you are redirected to third-party sites (e.g., viewing a presentation video hosted on YouTube or elsewhere), these third parties may however place cookies under their own responsibility. In this case, we invite you to consult the privacy policies of these external platforms.

9.3 Consent management and cookie settings

When you first visit our website or application, an information banner invites you to set your choices regarding non-essential cookies. You have the option to accept, refuse or customize the cookies that will be placed on you. Your choice will be retained and can be changed at any time thereafter. To adjust your preferences, you can:

  • Use the cookie management module provided on our site (for example, a "Cookies" or "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer allows you to reopen the consent panel and change your choices).
  • Configure your internet browser to block or delete cookies. Most browsers offer settings to refuse all or some cookies, or to receive a notification before a cookie is stored. Consult the "Help" or "Settings" section of your browser to learn more about cookie management. You can generally delete all cookies already stored via your browser. Warning: if you disable all cookies via your browser (particularly necessary cookies), certain features of our services may no longer work properly (e.g., unable to stay logged into your account, degraded display, etc.). We therefore advise you to keep at least strictly necessary cookies.

JEMA respects your choices regarding cookies. A refusal of analytical cookies will not result in any such collection and will not affect your access to basic features. If you accept certain cookies and refuse others, we will apply these settings. Note that your consent to cookies (or refusal) is recorded by means of a technical cookie for this purpose; if you delete your cookies or change terminal/browser, we will need to ask you for your preferences again.

9.4 Cookie retention period

Cookies each have a specific lifespan. In accordance with CNIL recommendations, non-essential cookies (e.g., analytics) that you have accepted have a limited validity period (in general, we do not use analytical cookies with a duration exceeding 13 months without renewal of consent). Strictly necessary cookies may be session cookies (automatically deleted when you close your browser or log out) or have a slightly longer duration if this is essential (e.g., a language preference cookie that may last a few days or weeks). In all cases, JEMA sets reasonable retention periods for its cookies, and refrains from exploiting them beyond these periods. You will find, in the cookie manager or upon request, the detailed list of cookies used with their exact purposes and durations.

12. Data Security

JEMA implements technical and organizational security measures to protect personal data against any loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. We take information security very seriously and strive to adopt industry best practices, in particular:

Encryption: Communications between your device and our services are systematically protected by encryption (TLS/SSL encryption) to prevent interception of your data in transit. In addition, sensitive data stored server-side (e.g., passwords, authentication tokens, certain account data or content) is encrypted at rest using robust algorithms. For example, passwords are hashed and salted in the database, so that they cannot be recovered in clear text.

Access control: Internally, access to personal data is strictly limited to JEMA employees who need it to perform their duties (need-to-know principle). Each employee with access to data is subject to a contractual confidentiality obligation and undergoes data protection training. Access to information systems is protected by strong passwords, multi-factor authentication where possible, and is subject to audit logs.

Secure infrastructure: Our servers are hosted in highly secure data centers located in France/EU, with first-class physical (access control to premises, surveillance, electrical redundancy) and logical (firewall, intrusion detection systems) protections. We perform regular encrypted backups of data to prevent any risk of accidental loss or alteration, and test the ability of our systems to restore data availability in the event of a physical or technical incident (in accordance with GDPR art. 32).

Secure development: Our software and applications are developed according to processes integrating security from design. We perform internal tests (and call on occasional external audits) to identify and correct potential vulnerabilities (e.g., penetration tests, static code analysis, etc.). Security updates for JemaOS and our applications are deployed regularly to protect your devices and data against new threats.

"Privacy by Design" approach: As mentioned in Section 5, JEMA natively integrates principles of data minimization and confidentiality. For example, many operations are performed locally on your device rather than on our servers, which reduces data exposure. Where server-side processing of personal data is necessary, we strive to use by default pseudonymous identifiers or aggregations when nominative detail is not required.

Despite all these precautions, no system is 100% infallible. JEMA undertakes to react quickly in case of suspected security breach concerning personal data. In particular, in accordance with the GDPR, we will notify the CNIL of any data breach likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of users within 72 hours of its detection, and we will also inform the persons concerned when this breach is likely to result in a high risk for them (e.g., leak of sensitive data). We also encourage you to maintain the confidentiality of your access credentials and your devices (e.g., do not share your password, use a PIN or fingerprint to protect the JEMA device) to contribute to the security of your data.
